# What we need from the three of us

Everything technical is handled. The system is built, it works, and it is on the internet. The only thing missing is **our actual clients** — right now it is running on invented companies.

There are no roles here. Nobody is being asked for anything different from anyone else.

---

## The ask, in one line

**Send the name of every company you have been to, and one or two lines about what happened.**

That is it. If that is genuinely all you can remember, that alone makes the system real.

---

Copy this into WhatsApp as-is.

> Send me a list of every company you've been to or contacted — even the ones that went nowhere, especially those.
>
> For each one, just:
>
> **1. The company name**

> – What we offered them and any price you quoted

>

---

## Why the DEAD ones matter most

This is the reason the whole thing exists. If Aboodi got thrown out of a restaurant in Jeddah and nobody wrote it down, one of us will walk into that same restaurant in three months.

When a company is marked dead, the system physically blocks the other two from adding it, shows them who closed it and the reason, in red, before they can go anywhere near it.

**A dead company with a blunt reason is worth more to us than a live one with a full contact card.**
